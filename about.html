Our Foundation: A 35+ Year Heritage of Integrity

Built on an unyielding commitment to client trust, disciplined processes, and long-term financial stewardship.

Founded in 1985 by our visionary founder, Mr. Gobind Singh Dhawda, Meera Associates began with a fundamental belief that financial services must be built on uncompromising integrity, genuine client relationships, and deep market insight.

When Jasbir Singh Dhawda stepped into the business in 1999, he carried forward the principles, discipline, and strategic vision passed down by his father.

Over the past four decades, the financial landscape has transformed significantly, but the core values of Meera Associates have remained constant.
