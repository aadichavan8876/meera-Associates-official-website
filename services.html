Step-by-Step Guide

How to Buy Shares in Demat

A transparent, 5-step process ensuring total security and direct electronic credit into your Demat account.

01

Share Selection & Valuation Inquiry

Specify your target unlisted company, quantity, and budget with our private market advisory desk.

02

Deal Finalization & Price Confirmation

Receive real-time market pricing, block size availability, and formal transaction confirmation.

Demat & CML Verification

Seller Protocol

How to Sell Shares in Demat

Liquidate your unlisted share holdings with transparent valuation, swift execution.

01

Holdings Portfolio Submission

Provide company name, total quantity available for sale, and proof of Demat holding statement.

02

Market Valuation & Offer Price

Receive optimal buyback quote based on prevailing private equity demand and order book depth.

03

Seller CML & DIS Documentation

Verify seller identity with CML and prepare Delivery Instruction Slip (DIS) or e-DIS authorization.

04

Off-Market Share Transfer

Transfer specified shares from your Demat account to Meera Associates designated clearing Demat ID.

05

Same-Day Payout & Confirmation

Direct bank transfer payout credited directly into seller's registered bank account immediately upon transfer verification.

💸 Target Payout Timeline: Payout initiated within 24 Hours upon verified receipt of Demat share transfer.
Depository Compliance

Stamp Duty & Regulatory Information

As per Indian Depository Regulations and SEBI guidelines, stamp duty is mandatory on all off-market equity transfers. Stamp duty must be paid prior to executing the Delivery Instruction Slip (DIS).

NSDL Stamp Duty Portal

Official NSDL online portal for e-DIS authorization and off-market stamp duty payment processing.
