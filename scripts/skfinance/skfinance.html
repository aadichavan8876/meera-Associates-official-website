🏦 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Financial Inclusion & NBFC

SK Finance Limited (formerly known as Ess Kay Fincorp Limited) is a prominent RBI-registered, non-deposit-taking systemically important Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

The firm specializes in providing comprehensive financial inclusion solutions, vehicle financing, and structured business loans to underbanked and unbanked segments across semi-urban and rural India. Driven by robust internal credit assessment methodologies and expanding branch networks, SK Finance caters to retail borrowers, small business owners, and self-employed individuals, bridging credit gaps across multiple states.

The company's primary lending pillars include vehicle financing (commercial vehicles, pre-owned and new cars, tractors, and two-wheelers) and secured micro, small and medium enterprise (MSME) business loans. The company has scaled robustly through capital infusions from marquee private equity and global institutional investors, including TPG Capital, Norwest Venture Partners, Baring India, and Evolvence India.

Systemically Important Institutional NBFC: Over 30 years of retail lending track record across semi-urban and rural India, backed by marquee global private equity investors like TPG Capital and Norwest.
Multi-Pillar Secured Credit Model: Comprehensive secured lending portfolios spanning commercial vehicles, tractors, two-wheelers, and secured MSME enterprise funding.
