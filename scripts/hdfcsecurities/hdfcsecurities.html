What happens to my HDFC Securities unlisted shares if the company announces an IPO? ▾

When HDFC Securities files its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) and lists on the stock exchange (NSE / BSE), your unlisted Demat shares automatically transition into publicly traded listed shares under the company's designated exchange ticker. Under SEBI ICDR regulations, all pre-IPO equity shares held by non-promoters are subject to a mandatory 6-month lock-in period from the commercial listing date, after which they can be freely traded on the public exchange.

How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling HDFC Securities unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
Are HDFC Securities unlisted shares eligible for corporate dividends and bonus shares? ▾

Yes, 100%. As an unlisted equity shareholder recorded under ISIN INE700G01014 in the depository register (NSDL / CDSL), you enjoy complete shareholder rights under the Companies Act, 2013. Any corporate dividends declared by HDFC Securities are credited directly to your bank account registered with your Demat account via ECS/NEFT, and bonus shares or stock splits are credited directly into your Demat account as per corporate record dates.
