📊 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Institutional Capital Markets

Prabhudas Lilladher Advisory Services Limited (operating as part of the PL Capital Group) is a premier Indian financial services and advisory institution with a prestigious legacy extending back to the founding roots of the broader Prabhudas Lilladher brand in 1944.

The firm specializes in institutional equities, bespoke private wealth management, retail broking, distribution of third-party financial products, investment banking, portfolio management services (PMS), and alternative investment funds (AIFs). Driven by technology-backed research, fundamental analysis, and deep market expertise, PL Capital caters to retail investors, high-net-worth individuals (HNIs), family offices, corporate treasuries, and domestic and foreign institutional investors (DIIs/FPIs).

Quantitative Investment Strategies (QIS): Advanced proprietary quant trading engines and multi-asset dynamic portfolio (MADP) frameworks that systematically manage asset allocation and optimize alpha generation across market cycles.
High-Performance Research & Advisory Terminals: Deep institutional equity coverage across 150+ stocks, fundamental sector reports, macroeconomic analysis, and corporate governance stewardship.
