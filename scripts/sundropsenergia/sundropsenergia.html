☀️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Solar IPP & Turnkey EPC

Sun Drops Energia Limited (incorporated in May 2019) is a rapidly growing Indian renewable energy enterprise operating as the specialized solar and hybrid energy arm of Surat-headquartered KP Group.

Sun Drops Energia uniquely bridges the renewable landscape by functioning simultaneously as an Independent Power Producer (IPP) that owns and operates utility-scale power assets, and a turnkey Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) provider.

The company focuses heavily on empowering commercial and industrial (C&I) clients to transition away from fossil-fuel-driven grids. By deploying customized captive power plants and long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), it ensures predictable recurring cash flows and high energy self-reliance for manufacturing businesses.

Backed by the robust engineering lineage of the KP Group, Sun Drops has steadily expanded into Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and large-scale green energy consolidation—positioning itself at the forefront of India's round-the-clock (RTC) clean energy transition.

Dual IPP & Turnkey EPC Execution: Integrated business architecture delivering captive solar infrastructure for industrial consumers alongside owning long-term revenue-generating IPP energy assets.
Landmark GUVNL BESS Storage Win: Contracted for a massive 450 MW / 900 MWh Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project by Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited, advancing utility grid stabilization.
