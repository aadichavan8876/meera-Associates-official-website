♻️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Clean-Tech & Mineral Security

Evergreen Recyclekaro (India) Limited (widely known as Recyclekaro) is one of India's leading sustainability-driven and advanced recycling companies, specializing in e-waste management, electronic scrap processing, and lithium-ion battery recycling. The firm focuses on recovering high-purity critical metals and strategic minerals—such as cobalt, lithium, nickel, and precious metals—from discarded electronic hardware and energy storage systems.

Operating advanced processing and zero-waste recovery facilities in Maharashtra, Recyclekaro bridges a vital loop for clean energy supply chains, electric vehicle (EV) ecosystems, and consumer electronics by transforming hazardous waste into reusable industrial raw materials.

Driven by innovation and a commitment to a circular economy, the organization partners with premier research institutions like IIT Bombay to scale up indigenous critical mineral extraction and green metallurgy technologies. Through rigorous compliance, eco-friendly processing methodologies, and a robust national network for waste collection, Recyclekaro supports India's net-zero transition, resource security, and stringent environmental regulations.

Advanced Closed-Loop Hydrometallurgy: Zero-waste extraction infrastructure in Maharashtra recovering battery-grade lithium, cobalt, and nickel to feed domestic EV battery manufacturing corridors.
Academic Collaboration with IIT Bombay: Joint technological partnership with Indian Institute of Technology Bombay pioneering cutting-edge green metallurgy and high-yield secondary extraction methodologies.
