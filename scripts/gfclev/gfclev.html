🔋 Business Ecosystem & Products

Specialty Chemicals

GFCL EV Products Limited is a leading Indian pioneer in manufacturing specialized battery chemicals and critical intermediate materials for Lithium-ion batteries. As a wholly owned subsidiary of Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited (GFL) and part of the multi-billion dollar INOXGFL Group, the company is developing India's first integrated battery materials manufacturing facility in Gujarat to substitute imports and fortify the domestic EV and Energy Storage Systems (ESS) supply chain.

🧪

Battery Electrolytes & Formulations

Formulated electrolytes customized for high-energy density and high-voltage Lithium-ion battery applications across EV two-wheelers, four-wheelers, and commercial fleets.

⚡

Electrolyte Salts (LiPF6)

Indigenous domestic manufacturing of Lithium Hexafluorophosphate (LiPF6), the indispensable conducting salt powering global Lithium-ion battery chemistries.

🔗

Battery Binders (PVDF / PTFE)

Proprietary Polyvinylidene Fluoride (PVDF) and PTFE fluoropolymer binders engineered for cathode and anode electrode coatings, ensuring robust mechanical integrity.

⚙️

Cathode Active Materials (LFP)

Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) cathode active precursor materials delivering superior thermal stability, long cycle life, and cost competitiveness for Indian driving conditions.

GFCL EV Products Limited is an Indian manufacturer of critical battery materials for Lithium-ion batteries. How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling GFCL EV Products unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
