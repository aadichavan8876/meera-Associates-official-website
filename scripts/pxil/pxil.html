Company Overview Business Profile

Power Exchange India Limited (PXIL) Unlisted Share is a premier power market infrastructure institution in India, providing an electronic platform for trading electricity and related products.

PXIL was the first institutionally promoted power exchange in the country, aiming to bring efficiency, transparency, and innovation to the Indian power markets.

The exchange is promoted by key stakeholders, including the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and the National Commodity & Derivatives Exchange Limited (NCDEX), among others.
