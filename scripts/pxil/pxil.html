🏛️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Power Market Infrastructure

Meera Associates is a trusted broker and dealer of Power Exchange India Limited (PXIL) Unlisted Shares. PXIL is a premier power market infrastructure institution in India, providing an electronic platform for trading electricity, green energy, and associated market contracts.

PXIL was the first institutionally promoted power exchange in India, established with a clear mandate to bring efficiency, liquidity, nationwide transparency, and financial integrity to the Indian power sector.

The exchange is institutionally backed by leading Indian capital market cornerstones, including the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and the National Commodity & Derivatives Exchange Limited (NCDEX), together with leading institutional energy investors.

CERC Regulatory Framework: Operates under the rigorous oversight of the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) with standardized market contracts and transparent auction algorithms.
Comprehensive Market Products: Provides continuous electronic platforms across Day-Ahead Spot (DAS), Term-Ahead Market (TAM), Real-Time Market (RTM), and Green Day-Ahead Market (G-DAM).
Renewable Energy Transition: Facilitates nationwide trading of Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) and Energy Saving Certificates (ESCerts), enabling DISCOMs and industrial leaders to meet statutory renewable purchase obligations.

With state-of-the-art technological clearing infrastructure and zero settlement default track record over 16+ years of operation, PXIL occupies a vital strategic position in India’s expanding national power grid ecosystem.
