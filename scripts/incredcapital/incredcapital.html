🏛️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Financial Powerhouse

INCRED CAPITAL FINANCIAL SERVICES is the comprehensive institutional equities, investment banking, and ultra-high-net-worth wealth advisory arm of the diversified financial powerhouse InCred Group.

The firm delivers institutional-grade equity research, capital syndication, structured credit solutions, and private market advisory to high-net-worth individuals, family offices, and leading corporate enterprises.

With robust balance sheet strength and seasoned leadership across premier global investment banks, InCred Capital bridges growth-stage private enterprises with domestic and international institutional capital pools.

Scale & Track Record: Managing over ₹30,000+ Cr across group credit and wealth assets with pan-India coverage across Mumbai BKC, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru.
Pre-IPO & Primary Markets: High-velocity franchise across curated primary allocations for high-growth tech & manufacturing unicorns and secondary liquidity matchmaking.
Compliant Custody: Safe off-market depository transfer execution (NSDL / CDSL) between consenting parties with complete regulatory transparency.

InCred operates integrated desks across wealth management, debt syndication, asset management, and equity capital markets, positioning it as one of India's fastest-expanding modern financial houses.
