☀️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Clean Energy & Solar Tech

Cosmic PV Power Limited is one of India's fast-growing solar photovoltaic (PV) module manufacturing companies, dedicated to driving the transition toward clean, renewable energy. The company specializes in designing, developing, and producing a diverse suite of high-efficiency solar modules, including Mono PERC and advanced G-12RN TopCon modules.

Catering extensively to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) contractors, and utility-scale green energy projects, Cosmic PV Power operates advanced manufacturing facilities equipped with automated production systems, stringers, multi-bus bar technology, and high-precision sun simulators.

With a robust distribution network spanning multiple Indian states, the company is deeply committed to supporting India's national solar energy targets and carbon-neutrality initiatives. By emphasizing stringent quality control, automated assembly lines, and continuous technological upgrades through its subsidiary and in-house execution frameworks, Cosmic PV Power delivers reliable, durable, and high-performance solar energy solutions tailored for diverse climatic and industrial environments.

High-Precision Automated Lines: Equipped with automated multi-bus bar stringers, robotic lamination, and Class-AAA sun simulators ensuring top-tier module efficiency and long-term field durability.
Comprehensive Module Portfolio: Indigenous manufacturing of Mono PERC and cutting-edge G-12RN TOPCon modules serving utility, commercial, rooftop, and OEM solar developers across India.
