🏛️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

National Exchange Operator

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI), formerly known as MCX Stock Exchange (MCX-SX), is a national-level stock exchange recognized by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

MSEI was established to provide an efficient, transparent, and hi-tech electronic platform for trading a diverse range of financial instruments. The exchange is known for its focus on advanced technology and a robust, fault-tolerant infrastructure to ensure fast order routing, immediate trade execution, and strong risk management.

It is a full-service national level stock exchange with licenses to operate across major market segments. Its flagship index is SX40, a free-float based index consisting of 40 large-caps, liquid stocks representing diverse sectors.

Marquee Sovereign & Institutional Backing: Key institutional shareholding held by State Bank of India (SBI), Bank of Baroda, HDFC Bank, and veteran marquee market investors.
Comprehensive Multi-Asset Licensure: Full statutory clearance from SEBI across Equities, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, and Debt clearing mechanisms.

What happens to my Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India unlisted shares if the company announces an IPO? ▾

When Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India files its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) and lists on the stock exchange (NSE / BSE), your unlisted Demat shares automatically transition into publicly traded listed shares under the company's designated exchange ticker. Under SEBI ICDR regulations, all pre-IPO equity shares held by non-promoters are subject to a mandatory 6-month lock-in period from the commercial listing date, after which they can be freely traded on the public exchange.

How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
Are Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India unlisted shares eligible for corporate dividends and bonus shares? ▾

Yes, 100%. As an unlisted equity shareholder recorded under ISIN INE312K01010 in the depository register (NSDL / CDSL), you enjoy complete shareholder rights under the Companies Act, 2013. Any corporate dividends declared by Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India are credited directly to your bank account registered with your Demat account via ECS/NEFT, and bonus shares or stock splits are credited directly into your Demat account as per corporate record dates.
