🌱 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Renewable IPP Infrastructure

Inox Neo Energies Limited (INEPL) is the renewable-energy Independent Power Producer (IPP) arm of the multi-billion-dollar INOXGFL Group. The company specializes in developing, building, and operating utility-scale wind, solar, and wind-solar hybrid power projects, supplying clean power under long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) to central and state power procurement agencies as well as high-credit corporate commercial clients.

Operating under the broader clean energy vertical of the group alongside Inox Solar Ltd, Inox Neo leverages strong internal synergies across the entire renewable value chain: wind turbine manufacturing and technology via Inox Wind, solar EPC engineering, and lifecycle operations & maintenance (O&M) via Inox Green Energy Services.

Advanced Hybrid Wind & Solar Parks: Deploys cutting-edge wind turbine generators, modern bifacial solar PV arrays, and shared high-voltage hybrid evacuation substations designed to maximize grid stability, annual energy yield, and plant load factor (PLF).
Digital Remote-Monitoring SCADA Architecture: Integrated supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) platforms ensuring predictive maintenance, real-time performance analytics, and uninterrupted power transmission across multi-megawatt renewable installations.
