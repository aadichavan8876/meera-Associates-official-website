🏭 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Heavy Metal & Infrastructure

Founded in 1932 as M/s Rajankumar and Bros. (Impex) by Shri Sevantilal C. Shah and incorporated as a corporate entity in 2013, RKB Global Limited has grown over an 85+ year operating lineage from a family-run iron and steel trading firm into an integrated industry leader.

RKB Global Limited is an established manufacturing and trading enterprise dealing in industrial metal and steel products as well as pre-engineered buildings (PEB). The company handles primary structural steel items including hot-rolled and cold-rolled coils, heavy sheets, and structural plates catering to critical heavy engineering, capital infrastructure, pre-fabricated construction, and industrial manufacturing sectors nationwide.

Venturing into direct steel manufacturing in 2021 with state-of-the-art facilities in Wada (Maharashtra) and Talod (Gujarat), the company operates high-throughput manufacturing and warehousing infrastructure alongside regional sales and storage hubs across Madhya Pradesh, executing both hyper-localized and broad national supply chain mandates.

Manufacturing Facilities in Wada & Talod: Advanced manufacturing plants equipped with precision slitting, cut-to-length processing lines, automated roll forming, and structural fabrication machinery.
Integrated Iron Ore Mining & Export Reach: Active mineral handling, iron ore mining modernization, and bulk commodity trading supporting raw material supply security and cross-border export shipments.

How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling RKB Global unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
