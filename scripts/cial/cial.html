❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Investor Intelligence
What makes CIAL unique among Indian infrastructure assets? ▾
CIAL is India's first airport built under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model with over 38,000 retail/NRI shareholders and the Government of Kerala. It is also the world's first fully solar-powered airport, generating 100% of its operating power through captive green solar generation.
What is the official ISIN and Depository status of CIAL shares? ▾
The official ISIN for Cochin International Airport Ltd is INE02KH01019 with a face value of ₹10/- per equity share. The shares are fully dematerialized and eligible for settlement across both NSDL and CDSL depositories.
What is CIAL's dividend history? ▾
CIAL boasts one of the most generous and consistent dividend track records in the unlisted space, regularly declaring dividends exceeding 35% to 40% of face value backed by surging aero and non-aero net cash flows.
How are transactions in CIAL unlisted shares executed? ▾
Transactions are executed through compliant off-market depository transfers between buyer and seller Demat accounts via DIS slips or online CDSL Easiest / NSDL Speed-e, with strict Client Master Report (CMR) verification and bank settlement.
What are CIAL's primary revenue streams? ▾
Revenue streams comprise Aeronautical revenues (landing fees, parking fees, UDF/PSF) and high-margin Non-Aeronautical revenues (duty-free operations, retail leasing, F&B concessions, solar power supply, cargo handling, and hotel hospitality).
Can I sell my existing Cochin International Airport shares, and how fast is payout credited? ▾

Yes, guaranteed same-day bank credit. Meera Associates actively purchases unlisted equity shares of Cochin International Airport. Once you initiate the off-market depository transfer to our designated clearing Demat account via CDSL Easiest or NSDL, funds are credited directly to your bank account via RTGS/NEFT on the exact same business day upon transfer confirmation.

What happens to my Cochin International Airport unlisted shares if the company announces an IPO? ▾

When Cochin International Airport files its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) and lists on the stock exchange (NSE / BSE), your unlisted Demat shares automatically transition into publicly traded listed shares under the company's designated exchange ticker. Under SEBI ICDR regulations, all pre-IPO equity shares held by non-promoters are subject to a mandatory 6-month lock-in period from the commercial listing date, after which they can be freely traded on the public exchange.

How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling Cochin International Airport unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
