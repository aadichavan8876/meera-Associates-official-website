🏭 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Industrial & Medical Infrastructure

Inox Air Products Limited is one of India's largest and leading producers of industrial and medical gases. Operating as a premier joint venture between the Jain family's Inox Group and the US-based Air Products and Chemicals Inc. (NYSE: APD), the company specializes in manufacturing, liquefying, and supplying ultra-high-purity gases including oxygen, nitrogen, argon, hydrogen, helium, carbon dioxide, and specialized gas mixtures.

Catering to critical core sectors such as steel manufacturing, healthcare systems, basic chemicals, pharmaceuticals, glass, and advanced automotive manufacturing, Inox Air Products operates an extensive pan-India network of cryogenic air separation units (ASUs) and dedicated cryogenic liquid distribution logistics.

The company is actively investing in next-generation green hydrogen infrastructure, low-carbon industrial solutions, and energy-efficient plant engineering to support the nation's decarbonization targets and promote sustainable heavy industrial practices.

Pan-India Cryogenic ASUs & Bulk Pipelines: Massive network of air separation units co-located at client facilities alongside cryogenic fleet pipelines ensuring uninterrupted bulk supply to national steel and manufacturing majors.
Remote Telemetry & Automated Logistics: Utilizes remote telemetry monitoring systems on customer storage tanks and automated supply chain tracking to optimize inventory management, ensure zero stock-outs, and maintain the highest benchmarks of operational safety.

How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling Inox Air Products unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
