☀️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Clean Energy Architecture

Goldi Solar Limited (formerly known as Goldi Sun Private Limited) is one of India's leading solar photovoltaic (PV) module manufacturers and a comprehensive clean energy solutions provider. Headquartered in Gujarat with world-class automated manufacturing facilities in Surat, the company specializes in producing high-efficiency solar panels, undertaking utility-scale engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) projects, and offering independent power production (IPP) services.

Catering to robust domestic and global clean energy markets, Goldi Solar plays a vital role in accelerating India’s transition toward sustainable renewable energy adoption. Driven by a vision to make green energy accessible, affordable, and reliable, the company places heavy emphasis on continuous technological innovation, producing high-wattage, high-efficiency solar panels that cater to diverse segments including rooftop installations, commercial and industrial (C&I) clients, agricultural water pumps, and utility-scale solar power parks.

Automated High-Capacity Module Lines: Operates modern, automated manufacturing lines equipped with cutting-edge mono-facial and bi-facial PV module production technology and rigorous automated quality-testing laboratories.
Turnkey Utility & EPC Framework: Proven execution track record delivering end-to-end grid-connected solar power plants, institutional rooftop arrays, and off-grid solar pumping installations across India and international markets.

Core Offerings
⚡

Solar PV Modules

High-efficiency multi-crystalline, mono-PERC, and N-type TOPCon photovoltaic modules catering to residential, commercial, industrial, and utility-scale solar power installations.

🏗️

EPC & Turnkey Solutions

Comprehensive design, engineering, procurement, installation, commissioning, and long-term operations & maintenance (O&M) services for utility-scale solar power plants.

💧

Solar Water Pumping Systems

Eco-friendly solar-powered pumping solutions engineered to empower agricultural irrigation, rural electrification, and government off-grid schemes (such as PM-KUSUM).

🏢

Commercial & Industrial Solutions

Tailored rooftop and open-access captive solar power systems enabling commercial businesses and industrial hubs to drastically reduce operational power tariffs.
