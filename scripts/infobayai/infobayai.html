🧠 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Synthetic AI Infrastructure

InfoBay AI Limited (originally incorporated in 2016 as EduGorilla Community Private Limited) is a prominent Indian technology enterprise that has successfully transitioned from an education-focused SaaS and test-prep provider into a high-growth synthetic AI data infrastructure company.

After years of scaling its vernacular digital learning and test preparation ecosystem, the company executed a major business model transformation to focus entirely on artificial intelligence training data infrastructure. InfoBay operates as a specialized data refinery. Instead of building consumer-facing applications, it engineers foundational data layers—such as domain-specific synthetic datasets, expert-verified Q&A text pairs, multi-modal audio corpora, and rigorous post-training evaluation loops—to help global technology teams train and fine-tune large language models (LLMs).

Driven by its strategic pivot into high-demand AI workflows, the company scaled its operations rapidly, crossing major revenue milestones and establishing itself as a key supplier of clean, bias-mitigated, and factually accurate enterprise training data.

Specialized AI Data Refinery Architecture: Precision generation of high-fidelity synthetic datasets, domain-specific corpora, and multi-modal alignment loops accelerating enterprise LLM convergence.
Scaled Enterprise Supplier: Mission-critical supplier of clean, bias-mitigated, and privacy-compliant training inputs for global AI and machine learning engineering teams.
