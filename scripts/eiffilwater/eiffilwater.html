💧 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Environmental Engineering

Eiffil Water Infra Limited (EWIL) is a leading infrastructure development company in India, specializing in the design, construction, commissioning, operation, and maintenance of comprehensive water supply systems, underground drainage (UGD), sewage treatment plants (STPs), and agricultural irrigation networks.

With a rich legacy rooted in engineering excellence since 1998, the company has grown into a trusted partner for government bodies, municipal corporations, and urban local authorities across multiple states, including Gujarat, Rajasthan, Karnataka, and the Union Territory of Daman.

Holding prestigious credentials such as an "AA" Class registration with the Roads & Buildings (R&B) Division (qualifying the company to bid for government tenders of any scale without monetary ceiling) and equivalent PWD classifications, Eiffil Water Infra leverages deep technical capabilities, structured digital execution, and rigorous quality standards to deliver resilient public utility assets that support sustainable community growth.

"AA" Class Government Accreditation: Registered as an unrestricted "AA" Class contractor with the R&B Division, enabling unrestricted participation in mega-scale state and central infrastructure tenders across municipal and water utilities.
Strategic Public Missions: Active execution partner in nationwide flagship water missions including AMRUT 2.0 (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation) and the Jal Jeevan Mission for rural piped drinking water.

🚰 Products & Strategic Capabilities

Core Capabilities
🌊

Water Supply Systems

Comprehensive design and laying of bulk water transmission pipelines, distribution networks, overhead water storage tanks, and automation-driven filtration and treatment plants.

🏗️

Underground Drainage & Sewerage (UGD)

Large-scale implementation of deep underground sewage networks, pumping stations, and advanced Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) to manage municipal wastewater efficiently.

🌾

Irrigation Infrastructure

Execution of wide-ranging canal networks, pressurized pipeline-based distribution irrigation systems, and micro-irrigation facilitation to support agrarian modernization.

⚙️

Operations & Maintenance (O&M)

Long-term lifecycle management, telemetry monitoring, SCADA operations, and corrective upkeep of commissioned water and wastewater treatment infrastructure.
