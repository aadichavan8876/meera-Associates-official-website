🛡️ Company Profile & Scale of Operations

Precision Defense Aerospace

JSR Dynamics Limited is an Indian pioneering defense technology company specializing in the indigenous design, development, and production of airborne guided weapon systems and precision-guided munitions (PGMs).

The company integrates advanced munitions with high-performance explosives while modernizing legacy rockets, missiles, bombs, and firing mechanisms for deployment across land, sea, air, and underwater platforms. Engineered to reduce reliance on foreign defense technologies, JSR Dynamics combines next-generation innovation with extended operational lifespans to meet evolving modern warfare requirements across domestic and international markets.

Operating advanced specialized manufacturing setups featuring 5-axis/4-axis CNC machines, laser welding, and high-precision testing infrastructure (such as Hardware-in-Loop and 3-axis CMM), JSR Dynamics meets stringent military-grade defense criteria. The enterprise focuses heavily on indigenous design, AI-driven object detection, flight trajectory simulation, and mission planning systems software integration.

Veteran Military Command & Indigenous Innovation: Founded by former Vice Chief of Air Staff Air Marshal Shirish Baban Deo and guided by decorated armed forces leaders to spearhead indigenous precision strike capabilities.
Advanced Aerospace Defense Infrastructure: State-of-the-art 5-axis/4-axis CNC machining, laser welding, Hardware-in-Loop testing, and AI-driven mission planning software suites.

R&D Pipeline

Developing rocket booster-assisted wing and EO/IR guidance kits to convert standard 250 kg gravity bombs into extended-range precision weapons.
