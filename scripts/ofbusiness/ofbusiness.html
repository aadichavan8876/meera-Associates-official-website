🌐 Company Profile & Scale of Operations

B2B Commerce & Financial Technology

OFB Tech Limited (popularly known as OfBusiness) is a tech-enabled B2B commerce and financing platform that focuses on raw material procurement, supply chain logistics, and credit solutions for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) across manufacturing and infrastructure sectors.

Specializing in core materials like metals, chemicals, building materials, agricultural products, and textiles, OfBusiness combines digital procurement infrastructure (BizFin) with financial credit extensions to streamline operations for businesses across India and international markets.

The company operates digital B2B matching marketplaces, AI-enabled supply chain workflow software (Nexizo), robust risk assessment models, and integrated financing frameworks designed to facilitate seamless trade execution, real-time raw material tracking, and smart inventory management for industrial enterprises.

Dual Commerce + BizFin Synergy: Uniquely combines high-volume industrial raw material distribution (metals, chemicals, polymers) with customized working capital credit lines for manufacturing SMEs.
Proprietary Nexizo AI Platform: Scaled AI-driven supply chain workflow automation optimizing pricing intelligence, order matching, freight tracking, and risk underwriting.

How is Capital Gains Tax calculated on profits from selling OFB Tech unlisted shares? ▾

Profits on unlisted equity shares are taxed under the Indian Income Tax Act based on holding tenure:

  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG): If held for 24 months or less, net gains are added to your taxable income and taxed at your applicable personal income tax slab rates.
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG): If held for more than 24 months, net gains are taxed at 12.5% without indexation benefits (as per recent Finance Act provisions).
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Not applicable on off-market transfers; statutory stamp duty @ 0.015% is paid at the time of depository transfer.
