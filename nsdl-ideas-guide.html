📋 Phase 1: Overview & Prerequisites

Checklist

NSDL IDEAS (Internet-based Demat Account Statement) allows beneficial owners to view live holdings, valuations, and transaction history. NSDL SPEED-e enables paperless electronic submission of Delivery Instruction Slips (DIS) for off-market and market transfers directly from your Demat account.

💡
Why use SPEED-e for Unlisted Share Selling? It completely eliminates physical paper DIS slips, courier transit delays, and signature mismatch rejections, allowing you to transfer shares directly to Meera Associates clearing account within minutes.

Keep These Details Ready Before Starting:

  • ✓ Your 8-Character DP ID: Starts with IN followed by 6 numbers (e.g., IN300126).
  • ✓ Your 8-Digit Client ID: Numerical 8 digits provided by your stockbroker / bank DP.
  • ✓ Registered Mobile Number & Email: Must match the contact records linked with your Demat account to receive verification OTPs.
  • ✓ PAN Card Number: 10-digit Permanent Account Number of the primary Demat account holder.

💻 Phase 2: Step-by-Step Portal Registration

Step-by-Step
01

Open the Official NSDL e-Services Website

Launch your web browser and navigate directly to the official NSDL e-Services gateway.

https://eservices.nsdl.com

On the home page under the left navigation menu, look for "New User Registration" and click on "SPEED-e / IDeAS" (or click the green "Register Online for IDeAS" button).

02

Enter Your Demat Account Credentials

A registration form will prompt you for your Demat identification details:

  • ✓ DP ID: Enter your 8-character DP ID (e.g. IN300126).
  • ✓ Client ID: Enter your 8-digit client account number.
  • ✓ Mobile Number: Enter the mobile number registered with your Depository Participant.
  • ✓ Captcha Verification: Enter the security code displayed on the screen and click "Submit".
03

Verify One-Time Password (OTP)

NSDL will instantly send a 6-digit OTP to your registered mobile number and email ID. Enter the received OTP into the verification screen and click "Continue".

⚠️
Did not receive OTP? Wait 60 seconds before requesting a resend. Ensure SMS delivery is not blocked by Do-Not-Disturb (DND) filters on your telecom operator.
04

Choose Your User ID & Password

Set up your secure permanent login credentials:

  • ✓ User ID: Choose an alphanumeric username (between 3 to 8 characters, e.g. rahul85).
  • ✓ Password: Create a strong password (8 to 16 characters containing letters, numbers, and at least one special character).
  • ✓ Confirm Password: Re-enter the exact password to confirm.

Click "Submit". You will receive an immediate on-screen confirmation acknowledging successful registration.

👥 Phase 3: Adding Beneficiary (Meera Associates Clearing Account)

Crucial Setup

Before transferring unlisted shares, SEBI regulations mandate pre-registering the recipient Demat account as a verified Beneficiary Account.

01

Log In to Your NSDL Account

Go to https://eservices.nsdl.com and click on "Beneficial Owner". Enter your newly created User ID and Password, complete the OTP login, and access your main dashboard.

02

Navigate to Beneficiary Management

In the top navigation bar, click on "Transfer / Beneficiary Services" (or "Beneficiary Maintenance") and select:

If Target Account is NSDL Click "Add Beneficiary NSDL"
If Target Account is CDSL Click "Add Beneficiary Non-NSDL"
03

Enter Meera Associates Clearing Account Details

Provide the designated clearing account information provided by your Meera Associates relationship manager:

  • ✓ Target DP ID: 8-character DP ID (for NSDL) or first 8 digits of CDSL BO ID.
  • ✓ Target Client ID: 8-digit client ID (or remaining 8 digits of CDSL BO ID).
  • ✓ Beneficiary PAN: 10-digit PAN of the clearing account entity.
  • ✓ Account Nickname: Enter Meera Associates Clearing for fast future identification.
🛡️
Contact Meera Associates for Exact Details: Please message or call our verified advisory desk at +91 89833 88881 or landline +91 84460 06200 to receive the official active clearing Demat credentials and Client Master List (CML).
04

Validate & Confirm with OTP

Click the "Validate" button. The NSDL system will cross-check the entered PAN and Demat ID to confirm that the recipient name matches official depository records. Click "Submit" and authenticate with the OTP received on your mobile.

⏱️
Depository Cooling Period: Once added, NSDL enforces a short cooling window (typically 30 minutes to 4 hours depending on your DP) before you can initiate the transfer to the newly added beneficiary.

⚡ Phase 4: Executing Off-Market Share Transfer

e-DIS Delivery
01

Initiate Off-Market Instruction

Log in to NSDL SPEED-e. Under the "Transfers" menu, click on "Off-Market Transfer / Delivery Instruction". Select your source Demat account.

02

Select Beneficiary & Stock Details

Fill in the transaction parameters:

  • ✓ Target Beneficiary: Select Meera Associates Clearing from your saved beneficiary list.
  • ✓ ISIN Number: Select or enter the exact 12-character ISIN of the company (e.g., INE127G01010 for NCDEX, INE235Q01028 for Rite Water, etc.).
  • ✓ Quantity: Enter the exact number of shares you have agreed to liquidate.
  • ✓ Reason Code: Select "Off-Market Sale" from the dropdown.
  • ✓ Consideration Amount: Enter total agreed transaction value in Indian Rupees (₹).
03

Stamp Duty Verification & OTP Authentication

In compliance with SEBI and the Indian Stamp Act, input your stamp duty payment transaction reference or pay directly through the integrated gateway. Review the transaction summary carefully and click "Verify / Authorize". Enter the final authorization OTP.

04

Download Transfer Acknowledgement

Upon successful authorization, NSDL will generate a unique Transaction Reference ID and a downloadable PDF Delivery Instruction Slip (DIS) receipt.
