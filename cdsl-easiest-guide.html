📋 Phase 1: Overview & "easi" vs "easiest" Distinction

Prerequisites

CDSL provides two levels of web access for account holders. Understanding the difference is crucial before registering:

CDSL "easi" (View Only) View holding statements and transactions only. Cannot transfer shares.
CDSL "easiest" (Transaction Enabled) Required for selling shares. Enables paperless off-market share transfers to trusted accounts.
⚠️
Always Register for "easiest" (NOT just "easi"): When registering on the CDSL website, make sure you choose "To Register for easiest". If you currently have an "easi" account, you can upgrade it inside the portal to "easiest".

Select "Trusted Account (PIN)" Registration Option

CDSL Easiest offers two registration types:

  • ✓ Option 1: Trusted Account (Recommended & Fastest): Allows transferring shares to up to 4 pre-mapped trusted Demat accounts using OTP and an 8-digit Easiest PIN. No digital signature token or physical hardware is required.
  • — Option 2: Account of Choice: Requires purchasing a digital signature certificate (DSC) e-Token dongle. Unnecessary for off-market selling to Meera Associates.

Keep These Details Ready:

  • ✓ Your 16-Digit BO ID: 8-digit DP ID + 8-digit Client ID (e.g. 1208160012345678) found in your broker app (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox).
  • ✓ Registered Mobile & Email: Must be active to receive OTPs during registration and transfer authorization.
  • ✓ PAN Card Number: 10-character alphanumeric PAN of primary holder.

💻 Phase 2: Step-by-Step CDSL Easiest Registration

Step-by-Step
01

Open the Official CDSL Easiest Registration Page

Access the official CDSL India registration portal directly using the link below:

https://web.cdslindia.com/myeasitoken/Home/EasiestRegistration

Alternatively, visit www.cdslindia.com, click on "Register Online", and select "easiest".

02

Enter Your 16-Digit Demat Account Number (BO ID)

Input your 16-digit Beneficiary Owner ID into the input field and click "Continue".

💡
Where to find your 16-digit BO ID? Open your broker app (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox), go to Profile -> Demat Details. Your 16-digit number is listed under "BO ID" or "Demat Account Number".
03

Verify via One-Time Password (OTP)

CDSL will send an OTP to your registered mobile number and registered email address. Enter the OTP and click "Continue".

04

Account Setup: Select "Trusted Account (Pin)" Mode

In the Account Details screen, configure your login credentials and transfer type:

  • ✓ Username: Choose a unique alphanumeric username (between 3 to 16 characters).
  • ✓ Security Question & Answer: Select a security question and type your answer.
  • ✓ Select Account Type: Tick the checkbox for "Trusted Account (Pin)". Do NOT select e-Token / Smart Card.

Click "Continue".

05

Map Meera Associates Trusted Account (Optional at Signup or Phase 3)

The portal will prompt you to enter the 16-digit BO ID of the Trusted Account you want to transfer shares to. Enter Meera Associates clearing account BO ID and authenticate via OTP. Click "Submit" to finish registration.

⏱️
Automatic DP Approval Window: Once submitted, your registration request is automatically forwarded to your broker / DP. Approval usually takes 24 to 48 working hours. You will receive an email confirmation from CDSL containing your activation confirmation and Easiest PIN.

👥 Phase 3: Adding / Linking Meera Associates Trusted Account

Beneficiary Addition

If you did not add the trusted account during initial registration, or need to add a new beneficiary, follow these steps inside your active CDSL Easiest dashboard:

01

Log In to CDSL Easiest Portal

Visit https://web.cdslindia.com/myeasitoken/home/login and log in using your Username and Password.

02

Navigate to "BO Linking Setup"

On your main top navigation bar:

Transactions → Setup → BO Linking Setup (or Miscellaneous → Edit Trusted Account)

Click on "Add Trusted Account".

03

Enter Meera Associates Clearing Account Credentials

Provide the designated clearing account information verified with our advisory desk:

  • ✓ Target Depository: Select CDSL or NSDL depending on clearing account instructions.
  • ✓ 16-Digit Recipient BO ID: Input the 16-digit Demat account number provided by Meera Associates.
  • ✓ Beneficiary PAN: Input the official PAN of Meera Associates clearing entity.
📞
Contact Us for Active Clearing Demat ID: Call our senior advisory desk at +91 89833 88881 or +91 84460 06200 to get the verified active clearing Demat details and Client Master List (CML).
04

Authenticate & Await DP Activation

Complete OTP verification. Your broker will review and approve the trusted account within 24–48 working hours. Once verified, the status in your portal will show as "Verified / Active".

⚡ Phase 4: Executing the Off-Market Share Transfer

Execution

Once your trusted beneficiary is Active, execute the transfer following these click-by-click instructions:

01

Go to "Bulk Setup" under Transactions

On the top navigation menu, hover over "Transactions" → click on "Setup" → select "Bulk Setup". (Bulk Setup is the standard option used even for transferring a single stock).

02

Create New Transaction

Click on the "Transaction" button (or Off-Market) and enter:

  • ✓ Execution Date: Select today's date (or next trading day).
  • ✓ Beneficiary Owner ID (BO ID): Select Meera Associates from your saved trusted accounts dropdown list.
  • ✓ Entity Identifier: Select "NA" from the dropdown.
03

Select ISIN & Transfer Quantity

Click on "Account ISINs". The portal will display your available Demat holdings:

  • ✓ Select ISIN: Check the box next to the unlisted company shares you are selling.
  • ✓ Quantity: Enter the exact number of shares agreed for sale.
  • ✓ Reason Code: Select "Off-Market Sale/Purchase".
  • ✓ Consideration: Enter the total transaction amount in Rupees (₹).

Leave fields like Exchange ID, Market Type, and Settlement ID blank. Click "Submit".

04

Verify & Commit the Transaction

The system will generate a Transaction ID. Select the transaction row, click "Verify", and then click "Commit".

05

Authorize with OTP & 8-Digit CDSL Easiest PIN

Enter the verification OTP received on your phone/email, and enter your 8-digit alphanumeric CDSL Easiest PIN (received via email upon registering for Easiest).

⚠️
Do NOT enter your 6-digit broker TPIN: Easiest requires your 8-digit CDSL Easiest PIN. If you forgot your Easiest PIN, go to Miscellaneous → Reset PIN to generate a new PIN in 1 minute.
