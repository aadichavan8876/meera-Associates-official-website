💻 1. NSDL IDeAS Online Registration

Part 1

Follow these step-by-step instructions to register your Demat account for NSDL IDeAS (Internet-based Demat Account Statement) on the official NSDL e-Services portal.

Details Required Before Starting:

  • ✓ 8-Character DP ID: Starts with IN followed by 6 numbers (e.g., IN300126).
  • ✓ 8-Digit Client ID: 8-digit numeric account number provided by your stockbroker or bank.
  • ✓ Registered Mobile Number & Email ID: Must match the contact records linked with your Demat account to receive verification OTPs.
01

Open the Official NSDL e-Services Portal

Access the official NSDL e-Services gateway using the direct link below:

https://eservices.nsdl.com ↗

On the home page, click on "Register Online for IDeAS".

02

Enter Your Demat Credentials

On the registration form, fill in the following details:

  • ✓ DP ID: Enter your 8-character DP ID (starts with IN).
  • ✓ Client ID: Enter your 8-digit numeric Client ID.
  • ✓ Mobile Number: Enter the mobile number registered with your Depository Participant.
  • ✓ Captcha Verification: Enter the verification code shown on the screen and click "Submit".
03

Verify via One-Time Password (OTP)

A 6-digit OTP will be sent by NSDL to your registered mobile number and email ID. Enter the OTP into the verification box and click "Continue".

⚠️
OTP Not Received? Please wait 60 seconds before clicking "Resend OTP". Ensure your mobile number is not blocked by telecom DND filters.
04

Create Your User ID & Set Password

Set up your secure login credentials:

  • ✓ User ID: Choose an alphanumeric username between 3 to 8 characters (e.g. user123).
  • ✓ Password: Enter a strong password (8 to 16 characters containing letters, numbers, and at least one special character).
  • ✓ Confirm Password: Re-enter the password to confirm.

Click "Submit". You will receive an immediate on-screen registration acknowledgement.

👥 2. Beneficiary Account Addition

Part 2

Follow these steps to pre-register a recipient Demat account as an approved Beneficiary Account on the NSDL portal:

01

Log In to Your NSDL Account

Go to https://eservices.nsdl.com ↗ and click on "Beneficial Owner". Enter your registered User ID and Password, complete OTP/captcha authentication, and access your dashboard.

02

Navigate to Beneficiary Maintenance

In the main navigation menu, see inquiries section ("Add Beneficiary").

If Target Demat is NSDL Select "Add Beneficiary (NSDL)"
If Target Demat is CDSL Select "Add Beneficiary (Non-NSDL / CDSL)"
03

Enter Beneficiary Demat Details

Fill in the recipient Demat account information:

  • ✓ Target DP ID: 8-character DP ID (starts with IN for NSDL, or first 8 digits of CDSL BO ID).
  • ✓ Target Client ID: 8-digit Client ID (or remaining 8 digits of CDSL BO ID).
  • ✓ Beneficiary PAN: 10-digit PAN of the recipient Demat account holder.
  • ✓ Account Name: Enter a recognizable label (e.g., MEERA CAPITAL).
🛡️
Contact Meera Associates for Exact Credentials: Call or WhatsApp our advisory desk at +91 89833 88881 or +91 84460 06200 to obtain the verified active clearing Demat account credentials and Client Master List (CML).
04

Validate & Confirm with OTP

Click the "Validate" button. The NSDL system will cross-check the PAN and Demat ID to verify that the account name matches depository records. Click "Submit" and authenticate with the OTP received on your registered mobile number.

⏱️
Cooling Period: Once added, NSDL enforces a short cooling window (typically 30 minutes to 4 hours depending on your DP) before the beneficiary account becomes fully active.
