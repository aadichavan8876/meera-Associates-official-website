GFCL EV Products Limited is a leading Indian manufacturer of battery chemicals and intermediate materials for Lithium-ion batteries. The company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited (GFL) and is part of the INOXGFL Group. It manufactures critical battery materials including battery binders, electrolyte salts, electrolyte formulations, fluoropolymers, and cathode active materials that support the electric mobility and energy storage ecosystem. GFCL EV is developing India's first integrated battery materials manufacturing facility, aimed at reducing dependence on imports and strengthening the domestic EV battery supply chain.

The company focuses on advanced fluorine chemistry, sustainable manufacturing, and innovation to cater to global battery manufacturers. Headquarters Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, with manufacturing and operational complexes primarily based in Gujarat.
