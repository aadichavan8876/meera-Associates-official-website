💻 1. CDSL Easiest Online Registration

Part 1

Follow these step-by-step instructions to register your Demat account for CDSL easiest under the Trusted Account (PIN) mode on the official CDSL India portal.

⚠️
Always Register for "easiest" (NOT just "easi"): CDSL "easi" is view-only, whereas "easiest" enables paperless electronic transfers to pre-mapped trusted accounts using an 8-digit PIN. Make sure you select "To Register for easiest".

Details Required Before Starting:

  • ✓ 16-Digit Demat BO ID: 16-digit numeric account number found in your broker app (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox, etc.) under Profile → Demat Details.
  • ✓ Registered Mobile Number & Email ID: Must match the records registered with your Demat account to receive verification OTPs.
  • ✓ PAN Card Number: 10-character alphanumeric PAN of the primary account holder.
01

Open the Official CDSL Easiest Registration Page

Access the official CDSL India registration portal directly using the link below:

https://web.cdslindia.com/myeasitoken/Home/EasiestRegistration ↗

Alternatively, visit www.cdslindia.com ↗, click on "Register Online", and select "easiest".

02

Enter Your 16-Digit Demat Account Number (BO ID)

Input your 16-digit Beneficiary Owner ID into the field and click "Continue".

💡
Where to find your 16-digit BO ID? Open your broker app (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One, Upstox), go to Profile → Demat Details. Your 16-digit number is listed under "BO ID" or "Demat Account Number".
03

Verify via One-Time Password (OTP)

CDSL will send an OTP to your registered mobile number and registered email address. Enter the received OTP into the box and click "Continue".

04

Account Setup: Select "Trusted Account (PIN)" Mode

In the Account Details screen, configure your login credentials and transfer type:

  • ✓ Username: Choose a unique alphanumeric username (between 3 to 16 characters).
  • ✓ Security Question & Answer: Select a security question and type your answer.
  • ✓ Select Account Type: Tick the checkbox for "Trusted Account (PIN)". Do NOT select e-Token / Smart Card.

Click "Continue".

05

Submit Registration & Await DP Approval

Review your submitted registration details and click "Submit".

Approval Timeline Typically 24 to 48 business hours
Broker Action Online electronic DP approval (no physical forms needed)

Once your broker / DP verifies the registration, CDSL will send an email confirmation containing your activation status and an 8-digit Easiest PIN.

👥 2. Beneficiary Account Addition (Trusted Account Linking)

Part 2

To transfer unlisted shares or map a trusted recipient Demat account, follow these steps inside your active CDSL Easiest portal:

01

Log In to CDSL Easiest Portal

Visit https://web.cdslindia.com/myeasitoken/home/login ↗ and log in using your registered Username and Password.

02

Navigate to "BO Linking Setup"

On your main top navigation bar:

Miscellaneous → Edit Trusted Account(Buyers DP DEMAT ID)
Miscellaneous → BO Linking → BO Linking Setup

Click on "Add Trusted Account".

03

Enter Meera Associates Clearing Account Details

Provide the designated clearing account information verified with our advisory desk:

  • ✓ Target Depository: Select CDSL (or NSDL if transferring to an NSDL clearing account).
  • ✓ 16-Digit Recipient BO ID: Input the 16-digit Demat account number provided by Meera Associates.
  • ✓ Beneficiary PAN: Input the official PAN of Meera Associates clearing entity.
  • ✓ Account Nickname / Name: Enter Meera Associates Clearing for fast future identification.
📞
Contact Us for Active Clearing Demat ID: Call or WhatsApp our advisory desk at +91 89833 88881 or +91 84460 06200 to get the verified active clearing Demat details and official Client Master List (CML).
04

Authenticate & Await DP Activation

Submit and complete the OTP verification. Your broker will review and approve the trusted account within 24 to 48 working hours. Once verified, the status in your portal will show as "Verified / Active" and the beneficiary account is ready for transfer operations.
